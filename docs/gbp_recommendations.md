# Google Business Profile (GBP) Local SEO Strategy for Private Firearm Instruction

To maximize local pack rankings and map search visibility in Fairfax, VA and Northern Virginia, implement the following Google Business Profile (GBP) configurations:

## 1. Create Dedicated Service Entry
1. Log into **Google Business Profile Manager**.
2. Navigate to **Services** under your profile menu.
3. Add a new primary/custom service titled: **"Private Firearm Instruction"**
4. Link/Associate URL: `https://livefireinstruction.com/one-on-one-instruction`
5. Description:
   > *"Personalized 1-on-1 private firearm instruction and custom pistol shooting lessons at the NRA Range in Fairfax, VA. Ideal for beginners and advanced shooters. Tuition is $75/hr for one student or $125/hr for two students."*
6. Set Pricing: **Fixed Price: $75.00 / Hour**.

## 2. Additional Secondary Service Items to Add
- **Private Shooting Lessons (Northern Virginia)**
- **1-on-1 Pistol Training (Fairfax, VA)**
- **Beginner Firearm Coaching**
- **NRA Range Private Shooting Lessons**

## 3. Local Citation & GBP Post Strategy
- **GBP Posts**: Publish monthly updates highlighting 1-on-1 coaching openings with a direct CTA button ("Book" or "Learn More") linking to `https://livefireinstruction.com/one-on-one-instruction`.
- **Review Generation Strategy**: Encourage students who complete 1-on-1 sessions to specifically mention keywords like *"private shooting lesson"*, *"1-on-1 coaching"*, and *"Fairfax NRA Range"* in their Google reviews.
